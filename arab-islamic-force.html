      Levantine political movement

      The Arab-Islamic Force is a religiously oriented armed movement in Syria and Lebanon favoring union with the Middle Eastern Federation as the means of ending the French mandate.

      It draws on urban ulema, mosque schools and guild networks. It welcomes the federal guarantee of religious establishment and sharia jurisdiction within the member states, while continuing to press for explicit Islamic references in any future Syrian constitution — the origin of its well-known reservations toward the secular character of federal codes. In 1936 it fields about seven thousand rifles. It cooperates operationally with the Levant Liberation Front while maintaining separate command.
