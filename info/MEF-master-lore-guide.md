## 1. High Concept [LOCKED]

The Middle Eastern Federation (MEF), commonly called the Federation, is an authoritarian federal republic founded at the end of the Turkish Civil War in 1923.

It is not a restored Ottoman Empire and not Ataturk's Turkey. It is a third path: a federation of autonomous states united under one flag, one army, and one head of state for the explicit purpose of ending European colonial domination in the Middle East.

As of October 1936 it has two members: Turkey Federal State and Kurdish Federal State (Kurdistan). Capital Istanbul. Head of State Mustafa Iodinm. It claims the Levant, Iraq, Yemen and portions of Armenia, with the French Levant as first strategic objective.

## 2. Point of Divergence and Civil War

### 2.1 OTL background for reference

OTL: Ottoman defeat 1918, Turkish War of Independence 1919–1922 led by Mustafa Kemal, Sultanate abolished 1922, Republic proclaimed 1923.

### 2.2 ATL civil war [LOCKED core, details PROPOSAL]

**[LOCKED]** Three-sided civil war, ending 1923:

1. **Sultanate forces** — restoration of Ottoman dynastic rule.
2. **Kemalist Nationalists** — Mustafa Kemal Ataturk's Turkish national movement.
3. **Iodinmist Federalists** — Mustafa Iodinm's cross-ethnic federal movement.

**[LOCKED]** Iodinm opposed the Sultanate, but argued a purely National Turk movement could not survive the might of colonial powers that would eventually re-subjugate the Middle East. Only unity of Middle Eastern peoples under autonomy guarantees could survive.

**[LOCKED]** Ataturk in MEF historiography is a liar whose historiography omitted negative facts. Iodinm exposed this and brought part of the revolution to his side, eventually including the Kurds, at which point subsequent military success convinced most of the population. Ataturk was eventually assassinated under strange circumstances by other nationalists.

**[PROPOSAL expansion — cut freely]**

- 1919–1921: Kemalists and Sultanists fight for Ankara/Istanbul. Iodinm, ex-junior officer turned rally speaker in Izmir and western Anatolia, builds cells among demobilized soldiers, merchants, and Kurdish notables.
- His rallies focus on three points: (a) Sultan betrayed the army in WWI, (b) Kemal will trade one master (Sultan) for isolation that France/Britain will crush, (c) only a federated army funded by all peoples can hold Anatolia and bargain with Europe.
- The 1931 revolt parallel: this rally method is later reused in Syria.
- Kurdish alignment 1922: Kurdish chiefs, fearing both Turkish assimilation under Kemal and abandonment under Sultan, accept Iodinm's written autonomy promise — keep language, religion, local law, local governors — in exchange for joining a united army. This tips the military balance.
- 1923: combined Turkish defectors + Kurdish fighters win decisive engagements **[leave vague for now]**. Ataturk loses control of part of his officer corps, then is assassinated by rival nationalists. Sultanists collapse without popular base.
- Federation is the initial proposal from day one of victory, not an afterthought. Revolution leaders immediately start constitution draft.

Bitterness of Ottoman defeat + colonial partition (Sèvres trauma, French in Syria/Cilicia, British in Iraq/Palestine, Greeks in Izmir) is the emotional fuel. People want an independent strong state *and* recognize necessity of autonomy for each culture inside it.

### 2.3 Why this POD works

- Explains why Turkey uses Turkish flag for Federation: victorious revolution kept 1923 flag as federal flag, each state keeps its own flag underneath **[LOCKED]**.
- Explains Turkish-Kurdish union without erasing tension: union is transactional (autonomy for blood), not idyllic brotherhood.
- Explains Arab sympathy later: founder is son of Arabs, movement was multi-ethnic from start.

## 3. Mustafa Iodinm (1892– )

### 3.1 Basics [LOCKED]

- Born 1892, Izmir, son of Arab merchant family.
- Drafted in WWI, ended war as officer (junior officer, not career pasha).
- Not necessarily a military genius; a uniter/agitator who became Head of State.
- Holds MEF Command (supreme command) because as Head of State he is always involved in federal affairs, not because he commands in field.
- Remains in office by broad respect across both states, but constitutionally removable by Council.

### 3.2 Early life [PROPOSAL to expand]

- Izmir in 1890s–1910s: cosmopolitan port, Greeks, Armenians, Turks, Arabs, Jews. Son of Arab merchants gives him Arabic at home, Turkish in school/street, Ottoman Turkish in army. Natural mediator.
- Merchant background matters: understands credit, logistics, bargaining — later federal bargain (autonomy for taxes/troops) is merchant logic.
- WWI draft: infantry, Palestine and Mesopotamia front where he sees Arab divisions and Ottoman collapse firsthand, plus French/British colonial troops. Returns convinced Ottomans cannot return and narrow nationalism cannot survive.
- Ends war as lieutenant/captain. No staff college pedigree unlike Orbay/Toyedemir — which is why he needs professional soldiers and keeps them close.

### 3.3 Rally period [LOCKED + PROPOSAL]

**[LOCKED]** Appeared as third leader, did rallies or something similar.

**[PROPOSAL]** 1919–1923 rally circuit: Izmir, Ankara fringe, Adana, Diyarbakir. Style: open-air speeches, pamphlets, veterans' associations. Core attack line:

> The Sultan sold you. Kemal will isolate you. The English and French will return with ships and loans. Only if Turk, Kurd and Arab stand under one flag with their own laws will we be left alone.

Exposure of Ataturk: Iodinm's group publishes captured orders / testimonies showing Kemalist exaggerations and reprisals against Kurds / rivals, positioning Iodinm as honest broker. MEF schoolbooks later call this “the Exposure.” OTL truth left ambiguous — perfect for wiki neutrality.

### 3.4 Character for wiki

- Age in 1936: 44. Young for a head of state, old enough for civil-war legitimacy.
- Languages: Turkish, Arabic, some Kurmanji, Ottoman military French.
- Religion: Muslim, presents as defender of Islamic tradition at state level while defending secular federal law. This duality is why Arab-Islamic Force both trusts and doubts him (see §15).
- Rule style: chairs Council, rules by decree day-to-day, defers to Marshal on operations and Chief of Staff on planning. Constantly touring states — hence “always involved.”

## 4. Founding of the Federation 1923–1924 [LOCKED]

- As soon as civil war ended, revolution leaders started constitution draft.
- Each separated state was going to keep their flag and autonomy.
- Final proposal took about a year to conclude (~1924).
- Federation was initial proposal, not compromise after failure.
- 1923–1924 is constituent year: committees on flag, army quota, tax, religion, language. Istanbul chosen over Ankara to signal imperial continuity without dynastic restoration and to use existing ministries, docks, arsenals.
- Keeping flags matters enormously for later Levant propaganda: “join and keep your flag” is offered to Syrians/Lebanese.

Governors are chosen by state, keeps autonomy in lore **[LOCKED]**.

### 7.2 Powers [LOCKED + PROPOSAL]

**[LOCKED]**

- Authoritarian with no general elections for Head of State.
- Pool of member states have regional autonomy and participate in central decisions.
- Iodinm can be removed if Council decides (10/14 rule below).
- Delegates are elected/selected (author said “elect 6 representatives”) — limited election for Council, not for Head.

**[PROPOSAL mechanics — approve/cut]**

- Iodinm rules by decree on day-to-day federal affairs, proposes war, budget, admission of new states.
- Council must approve: war, federal budget, new members, constitutional change.
- Removal: 10 of 14, Iodinm does not vote on himself. Effect: neither state alone (max 7: 1 governor + 6 delegates) can remove him. Both states must concur. Explains stability + theoretical check.
- Iodinm has tie-break in normal 7-7 deadlocks.
- Governors: internal administration, police, courts, schools, religion, regional tax; responsible for raising state quota for federal army and delivering recruits/animals/grain.
- Delegates: chosen in local assemblies ,to preserve “no general elections” feel while honoring “elect”.
- No term limits written; Iodinm stays by respect.

### 7.3 How it feels

OTL analogues: early US Articles + Yugoslav federal presidency + Soviet of nationalities, but Islamic-traditional legitimation. Authoritarian at top, bargaining at middle, autonomous at bottom.

## 8. Ideology — Iodinmism [LOCKED core]

**[LOCKED]** as defined by author:

- There was no way Middle East peoples would be free from colonial oppression if not under a united flag that allowed them to have their autonomy.
- Autonomy brought duties: participation in a united defense army.
- Idea not that different from initial United States.
- Not restoration of Ottoman Sultanate rule, but federation of administratively independent states united under single banner to resist oppression of West and colonial powers.
- Regions had important sympathy because idea of protection under strong Islamic state is appealing while still having individual autonomy.
- Federation secular as federation but allowed each state to have cultural/religious traditions.
- Slogan for wiki: “One Flag, Many Peoples.”
- Federal law secular (commercial code, military code, rail, customs). State law may be sharia-influenced, tribal, or secular republican depending on state.
- Economic corollary: federal customs union + Istanbul bank as shared assets.
- Educational corollary: Turkish + Kurdish + Arabic schools allowed; officer corps requires Turkish command language unless the whole corps is a corps from a different state.
- Foreign corollary: all French/British mandates are “unredeemed brothers,” but only Levant is actionable in 1936.

This duality (secular center / traditional states) is why Arab-Islamic Force trusts MEF as liberator but worries about religion — perfect internal tension.

- Languages: Turkish command, Kurmanji/Zaza in Kurdistan administration, Arabic in Izmir merchant networks and later Levant propaganda.

## 12. Military — German Counseling 1924–1936 [LOCKED]

- Start 1924 **[LOCKED, corrected from 1923]**. Germany did that to train and test weapons (OTL Lipetsk/Kama pattern).
- Intensified 1933 because Hitler saw emerging power as asset, country that could take old Ottoman role in war and keep Allies hands occupied in Middle East and Africa, and maybe even take part in Soviet invasion **[LOCKED]**.
- Counseling also exposed downsides of obsolete army **[LOCKED]**.
- While Iodinm and state leaders mostly focused on industrializing, in years leading up to war trainings and reassessments were conducted **[LOCKED]**.
- German mission in Ankara/Istanbul: infantry school, artillery school, small panzer-translation cell (explains Bozkurt I hybrid T-26/Panzer I-II).
- Soviet channel parallel: T-26s, P-6, artillery advice. MEF plays both Berlin and Moscow for hardware — very 1930s.
- British/French view: German officers in Istanbul are casus suspicion for France to refuse Syrian treaty, but the main reason is that Iodinm is very open about the intention to liberate the Middle East..
