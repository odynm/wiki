      Levantine political movement

      The Levant Liberation Front is a clandestine political and military organization in the French-mandated territories of Syria and Lebanon, sympathetic to the Middle Eastern Federation.

      sympathy dates to Iodinmist pamphleteering from 1925. After the failure of the 1931 rising, suppressed by the French authorities, surviving committees in Aleppo, Damascus and Raqqa federated as the Front. It maintains urban cells, tribal contacts on the steppe, and companies of riflemen with a small stock of machine guns and mortars, numbering about sixteen thousand effectives in 1936. Its program demands evacuation of French troops, union with the Federation under guarantee of the Syrian flag, and amnesty for the men of 1931.

      After the collapse of the Franco-Syrian negotiations in September 1936 the Front accepted federal liaison officers and coordinated its operations with the federation armies, alongside the Arab-Islamic Force and the Beirut organization.
